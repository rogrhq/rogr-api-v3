   - Call generate_queries_r1() or generate_queries_r2()
   - Replace arm["queries"] with new queries
6. Return diversified plan + config

  Intent-specific queries have LOW SIMILARITY to original claim
  - "water boiling point data" vs "Water boils at 100 degrees Celsius"
  - "water boiling point exceptions" vs "Water boils at 100 degrees Celsius"
  - Similarity likely < 0.4 (filtered out)
